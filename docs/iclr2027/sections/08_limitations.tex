\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

A decoder can become more accurate because it has stopped using the input the
claim is about. We showed three ways this happens and built a decoder in which
two of them are impossible. For the third, we made each stream's credit a
number that is measured, used to choose models, and reported for every result.
It shows that adding behavioural streams almost halves the brain's share of the
credit, and that on other public datasets, high accuracy can hide an imbalance
in the labels or a signal that cannot be confirmed as neural. Training against
the measurement did not raise the brain's share. That the measurement resists
this is the best evidence we have that it is measuring something real.
